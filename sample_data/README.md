# PAIMANA Sample Datasets & Provenance

This directory contains realistic synthetic infrastructure project records generated for testing and evaluating the PAIMANA platform.

---

## 1. Files Included

- `projects.csv`: 23 core infrastructure sanctions across Roads & Highways, Railways, Urban Mass Transit, Power & Renewable Energy, Water & Sanitation, and Ports & Shipping.
- `progress_updates.csv`: Monthly time-series reporting updates documenting planned vs. actual physical progress, expenditure vouchers, and documented field issues.
- `milestones.csv`: Work breakdown schedule deliverables, target dates, completion percentages, and status check-offs.

---

## 2. Realistic Domain Assumptions

1. **Physical-Financial Decoupling**: High-risk projects deliberately emulate real-world contractor distress where 60–80% of capital budget is disbursed while physical milestones lag below 50%.
2. **Sectors Represented**:
   - `Roads & Highways` (NHAI, NHIDCL)
   - `Railways` (DFCCIL, RVNL, NHSRCL, IRCON)
   - `Urban Mass Transit` (DMRC, BMRCL, MMRDA, GMRC)
   - `Power & Renewable Energy` (NTPC, NHPC, POWERGRID, THDC)
   - `Water & Sanitation` (Jal Jeevan Mission, NMCG, Ken-Betwa)
   - `Ports & Shipping` (JNPA, Vizhinjam, Vadhavan)
3. **Data Freshness**: Records simulate monthly reporting cycles up to the current operational calendar.
